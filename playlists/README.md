### How to use:
1. Copy the desired **Raw Playlist URL** from the table above.
2. Open your IPTV Player and choose **Add Playlist** -> **M3U Playlist URL**.
3. Paste the URL and set the refresh frequency to **Daily** or **Every 6 hours**.
